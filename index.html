01 · My story

Learning has never been about training for me.

It is about helping an organization make better decisions, develop stronger leaders, preserve critical knowledge, and create an environment where people can do excellent work.

My career began close to the work: building documentation, coordinating training, and solving the operational problems that make learning either useful or frustrating. Over time, my focus expanded from individual programs to the systems around them—governance, technology, measurement, operating models, capability frameworks, and executive alignment.

Today, I lead enterprise learning strategy and operations at Blue Origin. I work across functions to connect business priorities with the infrastructure and experiences employees need to grow, perform, and navigate change.

My best work lives where strategy meets execution: ambitious enough to change the system, practical enough to work on Monday morning.

Organizational structure and learning

What helps learning take root in small aerospace businesses?

My doctoral research examined the barriers, enablers, and strategic intent surrounding learning and development in small U.S. aerospace organizations.

The practical conclusion is straightforward: structure influences how learning happens, but leadership behavior, governance, and embedded systems determine whether it becomes sustainable.
